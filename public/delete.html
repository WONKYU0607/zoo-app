Account and Data Deletion

Zoo President · com.wonkyu.zoopresident

Last updated: September 27, 2026

How to request deletion

REQUEST ADDRESS

dnjsrb980607@gmail.com

  1. Send an email to the address above.
  2. Use Delete account as the subject.
  3. Include the nickname you use in the game. If you signed in with Google, also include that Google account address so we can verify it is you.
  4. We delete the account and reply within 7 business days of receiving the request.

Data that is deleted

Once the request is processed, all of the following is deleted. This cannot be undone.

  • Account — Google account address, account identifier
  • Profile — nickname, avatar, score, tier
  • Items — rewarded-ad tickets
  • Friends — friend list, friend requests sent and received, room invitations

Because the score and tier are removed, the account also disappears from the leaderboard.

Data that is retained

  • Rooms in progress — a nickname may remain in room and match data. That data is deleted automatically from the server within 10 minutes after the room ends. It is not retained beyond that.
  • No other personal data is retained after deletion.

Advertising identifier

This app serves ads through Google AdMob and uses the device advertising ID. That ID belongs to the device rather than the game account, so deleting the account does not remove it. You can reset or delete it yourself under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.
